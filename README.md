# Fingadget — Next-Gen Tech Journalism, Symposiums & Dharmic Cybernetics Platform

Fingadget is a responsive, multi-lingual technology journalism and long-form blogging platform engineered for Netlify deployment with a NeonDB PostgreSQL backend.

## Key Capabilities
- **Public Reader Experience (Zero Tech-Stack Jargon)**: Hero Bento Grid, Pulse Quick-Reads, Common Blog Feed with search, category pills, era filters, format filters, sorting, and pagination, plus HD murals and custom SVG clip-art motifs.
- **8 Dynamic Technology Era Themes & Banners**: Industrial Revolution, Transistor Era, Silicon Chips, Integrated Circuits (ICs), Apple & Personal Tech, Space Technology, Artificial Intelligence, and Quantum Computing & Subatomic Physics.
- **9-Language Translation Engine**: Instant full-site localization across English, Bengali (`bn`), Hindi (`hi`), French (`fr`), Spanish (`es`), German (`de`), Chinese (`zh`), Japanese (`ja`), and Russian (`ru`).
- **Editorial Admin Suite & Rich Text Formatting**: Secured by `ADMIN_USERNAME` and `ADMIN_PASSWORD` Netlify environment variables. Features font-family, font-size, color, bold/italic/underline, left/center/right/justify alignment, blockquotes, Dharmic verse blocks, live word/character counters, and Admin-only documentation.
- **NeonDB Integration (`fngdgt_` Prefix)**: Complete cleanup and schema/seed script at `sql/fngdgt_schema_and_seed.sql` and CORS-enabled serverless API at `netlify/functions/api.js`.
